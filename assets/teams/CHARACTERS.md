# Ultrack driver characters

The same person in every image. Lore (bio, background, personality, rivals) stays in `teams.json`; this sheet adds what a picture needs: the face, the build, how they carry themselves, the suit. For the twelve drivers with a poster the look is read from `track-viewer/public/assets/drivers/<slug>/illustration-v1.webp`; **Barreto, Ortega, Mizuno and Kuroda have no poster, so this sheet is the first definition of their look.**

Everyone here is fictional. Prompts never name a real person, team, car maker or sponsor, and the art carries no logos or lettering; type goes on top in the game.

Varrone's poster still shows the red Saetta suit from before he went home to Toro. From here on he wears Toro's yellow and black, and keeps the Saetta lightning bolt on his helmet (his lore).

## Style lock (first lines of every prompt)

> Original fictional character. Inked, cel-shaded digital illustration in the style of a crime-game loading screen: confident black ink outlines, flat graphic colour with hard-edged shadow shapes, warm golden-hour light, slightly exaggerated realism, editorial magazine composition. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no brand marks, no watermark anywhere in the image.
