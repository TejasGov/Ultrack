# V14 art pass (Blender review build, not exported)

`out/tokyo_v14.blend` is built from `out/tokyo_v13_1.blend` (never written) by `art_v14.py`, which runs three re-runnable passes in
one Blender process and bakes the tunnel's irradiance volumes:

```
powershell -File tracks/india/run_blender_locked.ps1 -Script tracks/tokyo/art_v14.py -Blend tracks/tokyo/out/tokyo_v13_1.blend [-Rest tunnel,lessons,street,nobake]
```

About 1 minute (passes 15 s, bake 12 s, load and save the rest). Shared code: `v14_common.py` (tunnel stations, review cameras,
BVH ray helpers), `v14_kit.py` (fractional-station sweeps and blocks, CC0 box-mapped detail, panel materials). Log:
`out/v14-build.json`. Nothing was exported: no GLB, `track.json`, `track-viewer/` or `out/web/` change. What must change at
export is in `V14-RUNTIME-MAP.md`.

Reviews: `render_v14.py` (EEVEE, 48 samples, 1600x1000, `-b -t 6` through the shared lock, never saves; `night` = the scene as
saved, `day` = noon HDRI and sun, emission x0.12, night-only collections hidden). Sheets: `make_sheet_v14.py` (ffmpeg) ->
`out/v14-review-tunnel.jpg` (rebuilt after the polish, with a day aerial), `-lessons.jpg`, `-street.jpg`, `-overview.jpg` (pre-polish). "Before" cells are the same cameras on v13.1.

## 1. The tunnel's "covered" problem

**What the audit found** (`inspect_tunnel_v14.py`, `probe_tunnel_v14.py`, `measure_tunnel_v14.py`; `out/v14-tunnel-audit-v13_1.json`,
`v14-probe-v13_1.json`, `v14-tunnel-measure.json`):

- The V10 box (`V10 submerged tunnel | 24m clear width`) is closed from the inside: of 118 covered stations, none lets a ray out
  (19 rays per station across the section). Clear height 6.8 m, liner face at 11.93 m.
- **Nothing sits on it.** Over the covered run (stations 1913-2148, 470 m) only the middle 214 m (1971-2078) is under the lagoon
  water. Over the two ends (about 120 m and 140 m) the terrain and the approach landscaping were carved down to the roof slab
  (top at -2.85; the landscaping falls to -3.3 at 14 m from the axis, and holes beside the walls reach -13.5). The tunnel was a
  slab lying in an open pit.
- **The only lid was a backdrop.** `V13 far ground disc` (the V13 far-land ring, a single-sided plane at z -0.9) was the first hit
  from above over the pit and over both open approach cuts. In Blender it rendered as a black ceiling over the approaches; in
  three.js its back face is culled, so from below there was nothing at all. That is why the approaches looked "covered" in the
  Blender reviews and open in the game.
- No headwall: between the roof top and grade at each portal there was a void. The ceiling was the box's untextured inner face,
  which rendered black.
- In the game, the sky light (image-based) and the low sun reach every surface regardless of geometry; the y = 0 mirror plane
  clipped the -12 m road. Those are renderer facts (`tunnel-light.js`, `mirror-height.js` already handle them); sealing the
  geometry is what the Blender source can do.

**What V14 builds** (collections `V14 | tunnel structure`, `V14 | tunnel cover and landscaping`, `V14 | tunnel fit-out`,
`V14 | tunnel lighting`):

- **Backfill mound** over both covered ends: a landscaped crest 32 m wide at grade beside each portal (3.25 m west, 3.84 m east),
  easing down to 0.35 m where it meets the lagoon, with 1:2.2 side slopes that run under the existing ground. Stone revetments
  (`japanese_stone_wall`) where it meets the water; a gravel path on the crest, low hedges, 26 sakura (linked copies of the
  lightest blossoming V3 sakura). The lagoon stays over the middle (2.3 m of water over a 2.35 m slab).
- **Portals**: a 6 m solid hood, a 1.2 m headwall with a 1.1 m parapet, a dark reveal and jambs from the liner into the banks,
  wing parapets along the cut tops for 16 m, and a name plaque (ひらがなトンネル / HIRAGANA TUNNEL L = 470 m; fictional name; the V13
  interior sign is renamed to match).
- **Light-screen louvres** over the first 48 m of each approach, at the tunnel's ceiling height plus 0.1 m, fins 0.6 m apart at
  the portal opening out to about 3 m: the transition zone, and the deep overhang the research asks for so the inside never shows sky.
- **Backdrop disc** cut out of both approach cuts (exact boolean, then the cutter's walls removed: 292 faces).
- **Interior**: painted soffit panels (2.4 x 1.2 m) under the roof; pale wall panels (1.2 x 0.6 m) with a dark lower band on the
  V13 liner (the Yamate photo reading; the tile material `V13 tunnel tile` is rebuilt); continuous upper-wall line luminaires on
  both walls (cool LED, `#e8f3ff`); entrance and exit threshold zones (two extra continuous ceiling rows for 40 m, one for 90 m);
  three pairs of jet fans (stations 1980, 2025, 2080), 1.35 m inspection walkway with a yellow and black nosing on the left wall;
  red fire cabinets 消火器 / 消火栓 every 50 m (8), emergency phones 非常電話 / SOS every 100 m (4), green lane-use arrows every
  120 m (4 pairs), an 80 km/h sign at the entrance. The V13 murals, niches, exit signs and the V13.1 luminaires are kept.
- **Lighting**: 24 wall-wash area lights to light the soffit, 12 threshold lights (custom property `v14_tunnel_zone`; the render
  dims them to a third at night), and 8 irradiance volumes along the covered run, baked, so EEVEE lights the interior from what the
  tunnel sees, not the open sky.

**Polish (2026-10-29, after the first review; the pre-polish file is `out/tokyo_v14_pre-polish.blend`).**
- The two yellow pools on the mound were not buried lights: three `07_STREET_KIT` street lamps (`Street lamp column.043/.044/.047`
  with their outreach and diffuser) stood 4-7 m in the air over the tunnel, left from the road profile before V10 lowered the road,
  and V14 had given each a sodium spot. `art_v14_polish.py` (run last) archives any street lamp whose foot is not on the mound
  (buried or floating) and deletes its V14 pool light (3 deleted), and would delete any light or archive any object under the fill;
  nothing else was found. Lights 1,521 -> 1,518.
- Grass (every material using the leafy_grass photo, i.e. the terrain, the extended lawn and the mound): a mown green from two
  low-saturation tones (`#6b8150` / `#5e744f`, luminance 0.19 / 0.16) mixed by 25 m world noise, times the CC0 photo at 35 %
  saturation divided by its own mean luminance (0.244), so the photo only adds blade and clump variation. Replaces the x0.42 tone.
- The louvres hid the headwall plaque from the approach: a 1.4 m name band (ひらがなトンネル / HIRAGANA TUNNEL L = 470 m) now runs
  on the outer fascia beam of each louvre screen, 6.85 m over the road, readable from about 100 m out.

Not built: an emergency parking bay (it needs the V10 box and liner cut open; the research found no dimensions either), an
evacuation passage, a ventilation tower.

## 2. Runtime lessons baked in

- **Dry asphalt**: `V4 damp Poly Haven asphalt` coat 0 (was 0.55), roughness from the ARM green channel remapped to 0.68-0.92
  (was 0.16), default roughness 0.85 for export.
- **Haze boxes**: the 4 `Localized red-lit bridge haze` boxes are in `V14 | _archive` (render-excluded, not deleted).
- **Neon**: 406 signs analysed (pylon boards per connected piece; tenant cabinets, fascias and frontage projecting signs per
  object). Each gets one palette hue (magenta 85, cyan 121, amber 59, mint 35, violet 52, warm white 54) and one style (232 lit
  panel with dark glyphs, 55 lit panel with white glyphs, 119 dark panel with neon glyphs); 404 glyph text objects follow their
  sign. Emission is luminance-compensated (2.2 x k, k 0.6-2.2) so the hue survives AgX; by day the panel albedo is tinted acrylic.
- **CC0 detail** on 29 flat materials (`out/v14-build.json` lists each with its set and scale): kawara (`grey_roof_01`, 2.6 m),
  cedar (1.4-1.9 m), lacquer (1.3 m), plaster (2.4 m), concrete (3.2 m; bridge towers 7 m), shutter metal (0.9 m), stone (4.5 m),
  paving (3 m). Box-mapped in object space, the albedo divided by the set's mean so every material keeps its own colour; roughness
  varies around its own value; AO and a bump from the AO channel. The grass photo is toned down (x0.42) so it no longer reads
  mint-white in sun.
- **Light pools** (`V14 | light pools`, 963 lights after the polish, no shadows): a sodium spot under each of the 53 remaining street-lamp heads, a warm point
  light per 9 m cell of lanterns (504), an area light in front of every sign in its hue (406).
- **Bridge**: 414 LED dots on the two main cables (2.6 m apart), 12 warm-white tower uplights, the V5 crimson washes turned warm
  white, the red only on the tower tops (two red lenses and two red lights per tower, `V14 | bridge night red`, hidden by day).
  The red uplight lens and deck edge materials are pale.
- **Night sky** (Blender world only): zenith `#070a1a`, horizon `#2a1840`.

## 3. Street identity (from `research/tokyo-street-research.html`)

- **Sign stacks** on the neon-street frontages (stations 470-790): 30 blade signs (tategaki, one character per line, both faces; 0.7-1.25 m
  wide, 2.4-7 m tall, at 3.4-12 m) and 30 flush tenant boards floor by floor, fictional generic shop words (居酒屋, ラーメン, 焼鳥 …),
  palette hues, 60 point lights.
- **Lantern alley** (ひらがな横丁, fictional): an Omoide-Yokocho-style lane at station 610, right side, from 17 to 44 m off the
  axis, in a free gap between frontage blocks (no object displaced): 11 smoke-stained timber stalls stepping with the ground, tin
  awnings, noren, warm counters, two strings of 34 chochin, a name arch and an 8 m name pylon that reads from the road.
- **Shuto deck**: the existing offshore Wangan-style viaduct gets sound walls (1.3 m aluminium, 1.9 m translucent), 36 sodium
  columns (orange against the circuit's white LED) and 3 green direction gantries with fictional names (湾岸ひらがな, 桜埠頭,
  月見台 出口). The V9 expressway signs that said 横浜 / 羽田 now read 月見台 / 桜埠頭.
- Not duplicated: holograms, the torii corridor, petals, the koi pond, sky glow and light-pool decals stay runtime parts.

## Weak spots

- The interior reads clean and bright but flat: the panels are a procedural pattern, not a photo set (the research could not verify
  the real lining), and the soffit has no service runs, cable trays or seams of the box.
- The mound is a smooth swept surface with hedges as boxes; the moat-like strips beside it are the lagoon water showing in the old
  landscaping valley.
- The louvres still hide the headwall plaque itself; the name band on the fascia carries the name instead.
- The alley sits 4.6 m below the road (the street is on a rise there), so from the car it is a glimpse over the barrier and catch
  fence: the pylon and the lanterns, not the lane. The stalls are boxes; no smoke, no clutter, no people.
- The blade signs are on the V10 frontages 25-30 m from the axis, so the street is wider than a real canyon; 30 blades do not yet
  make Akihabara density.
- The Shuto deck is 450 m off the circuit and reads only as a lit line from the start straight.
- Day: the aerial shows how much of the site is flat lawn; the grass variation is faint at that distance.
- Many light objects (1,521): fine for EEVEE previews, none of them exports.
