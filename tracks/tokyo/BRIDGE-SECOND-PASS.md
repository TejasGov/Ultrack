# Bridge second art pass

Scene: `out/tokyo_bridge_v5.blend`. The V4 source is preserved separately. Rebuild with `bridge_second_pass.py`, then `refine_bridge_v5.py` for the final lighting/framing adjustment. Verify with `check_bridge_v5.py`.

The user's suspension-bridge reference drives this focused revision: tall pale portals with a continuous deep rounded opening, a restrained dark suspension system, red uplighting, warm deck-edge lights and a dark night sky. Local illuminated humidity catches the tower light; it uses volume shading rather than a photographic smoke card. No cars or reference screenshots are embedded in the scene.

The portal opening is 24 m wide and 44 m high above the local deck, around the existing 18 m racing surface. Cables follow the existing curved and graded bridge segment. Surface textures, panel joints, anchor plates, hanger clamps and guardrail posts add detail at driving height. Bright overhead floodlight banks and high catch-mesh faces are removed within the bridge segment to reveal the tower silhouette. This is an environment-art model rather than a structural engineering design.

Review renders: `out/bridge_night_v5.png` and `out/bridge_approach_v5.png`. Lighting and atmosphere are Blender authoring effects; runtime baking and performance remain pending. The previous full-city scene and other districts remain available in V4.
