# Llano Speedway: sources

Textures from Poly Haven (CC0): aerial_sand (its pale copy in tracks/drylake/tex), asphalt_track (bleached, tracks/drylake/tex), concrete_wall_009, dirty_concrete, white_stucco, grey_roof_01, painted_metal_shutter, rusty_corrugated_iron.
Far range: the goegap HDRI (Poly Haven, CC0), cut by tracks/drylake/make_backdrop.py. Skies: qwantani noon and dusk (Poly Haven, CC0).
Glass facades: generated for Infinia (tracks/infinia/infinia_city.py). Crowd cards: generated for Shajawa (tracks/shajawa/build_shajawa.py).
Sponsor marks: assets/sponsors/logos, real marks, as the other tracks' boards (assets/sponsors/manifest.json has each source). Texas flag: public domain, drawn in make_textures.mjs.
Everything else is built in build_llano.py, llano_venue.py and make_textures.mjs.
Infield ideas from real US tracks, none copied: Laguna Seca (dry lake), Talladega and Watkins Glen (RV camping), Road America and Daytona (kart tracks), Talladega and Daytona (service tunnels), Las Vegas (media centre), COTA (fan stage), Barber (sculpture, footbridge), Sebring (airfield concrete).
Llano Central: built in llano_station.py in the grammar of New York's original Penn Station (McKim, Mead & White, 1910), a fictional building, nothing copied. Its surfaces: Poly Haven white_plaster_02, seaworn_stone_tiles, leafy_grass (CC0).
