# Venice: sources

Layout: the user's sketch (source/user_sketch.png), traced in plan_layout.py.
Textures from Poly Haven (CC0), in assets/polyhaven (tracks/venice/fetch_textures.py fetches the eight added for this track): large_sandstone_blocks, medieval_blocks_03, damaged_plaster, plastered_stone_wall, medieval_red_brick, brick_wall_10, clay_roof_tiles_02, roof_slates_02; and asphalt_track (bleached, tracks/drylake/tex), concrete_wall_009, leafy_grass, pavement_01, white_stucco, white_plaster_02, grey_roof_01, weathered_planks, yellow_plaster, red_plaster_weathered, peeling_painted_wall. Graded copies in tex/: make_textures.py.
Architecture ideas from real Venice, none copied: Doge's Palace, Arsenale gate, Rialto Bridge, Bridge of Sighs, San Marco campanile. Lower loop and city: ideas from the US Capitol, Manchester Town Hall, Washington's Federal Triangle, Altes Museum, Dresden Frauenkirche, Cologne Cathedral and the Holstentor (research/european-architecture-refs.md), none copied, no real names or emblems on any building.
A fictional circuit: no real motor race in Venice is claimed.
