# Venice: identity brief

Tokyo works because every part of its lap has things that could only be Tokyo; Infinia took the same rule for Mumbai. Venice follows it: a stylised lagoon city, our own place. No real building is copied, no real business name appears, and no real motor race in Venice is claimed. The upper half is Venetian; the lower loop is grand Victorian and European civic, then an older town behind it.

## Palette and atmosphere
- Pale Istrian limestone, ochre and terracotta plaster, weathered brick, clay tile, verdigris and lead on the domes.
- Accents: St Mark red and gold on banners, Venetian green on shutters, gondola black, striped paline.
- Water is always close: calm, reflective, with boats on it. Day and dusk only: no lit night shell.

## Race dressing, shared with the other tracks
Start gantry with lights, truss gantries with sponsors, catch fence by the stands, tyre walls and painted run-off at the big stops, 300/200/100 boards, corner names, hoardings, road rubber and patches, crowd in the stands.

## The lap, section by section

**1. Start and top straight (HQ, palazzi).**
- A paved forecourt before the HQ with a patterned stone floor and three tall flagstaffs on bronze bases.
- Red and gold banners on the lamps. Cafe terraces with parasols and tables on the walkway side.
- Palazzi get green shutters, window boxes, tall inverted-cone chimneys and wooden roof terraces.

**2. The climb to the deck (T1, T2).**
- Palazzi shop fronts with awnings and invented Italian signs: Caffè, Trattoria, Gelateria, Farmacia, Libreria.
- Painted street-name signs on the walls. Cypress trees marking the corner.

**3. The deck.**
- Cast-iron lamps on the parapets, stone urns over the piers, sponsor banners across the road on a light gantry.
- Mid-way, the Rialto span: the view over the lake on one side and the town on the other.

**4. The drop and T4.**
- Tyre walls and a big painted run-off, a stone clock on a post, a poster column at the outside of the corner.

**5. The bottom straight: the grand frontage.**
- A boulevard: plane trees and lamps between the road and the pillared buildings, poster columns and newspaper kiosks.
- Cafe terrace in the arcades. A paved square at the monument column, with a wellhead and benches.

**6. The hairpin (T5, T6).**
- Stand, tyre walls, painted run-off. Beyond it across the water, the dome hall on its island with a landing stage and boats.

**7. The canal (lower loop's middle).**
- Two stone arched bridges, moored gondolas with striped paline, a pontoon stop, water taxis.
- Laundry lines between the houses, window boxes, little squares with wellheads, shop signs at street level.

**8. The falling straight and the tunnel.**
- Stone cutting with carved panels, the Bridge of Sighs ahead.
- Inside: an old masonry tunnel. A brick barrel vault on dressed limestone walls over a sooted stone base, a stone rib every 20 m, warm light hidden along a ledge at the springing. Nothing painted: the user rejected a gold mosaic and star ceiling as comical (2026-11-03).

**9. The left side and the garden.**
- Lido garden: clipped hedge labyrinth, parterres, cypress avenue, pines, stone benches, urns on plinths, a small Art Nouveau pavilion by the lake. Row boats on the lake.

**10. The lagoon and the horizon.**
- Bricole channel markers leading to the island, a far band of campanile silhouettes, faint mountains by day.

## Left out by the user's choice (2026-11-03)
The tall ship, parked classic cars and scooters, and the Poly Haven street props were not approved for download; benches, planters and urns here are hand-built.

## Rules
- Each surface has a real photo set; generated art only where no photo exists (signs, posters, mosaic, flags), laid over a photo base.
- Nothing that reads as a placeholder close to the road. People are cards at most, and only where the track already uses them.
- No flicker, no hard blinking.
- Far objects get their own mesh (the web pack's 16-bit rounding); decals sit 5 cm or more over the road, panel backs 30 cm behind.
