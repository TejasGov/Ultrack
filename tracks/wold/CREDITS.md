# Hartwell Wold: sources

Layout: our own, planned in plan_layout.py (a country road circuit in the English Cotswolds). Hartwell is a fictional village: no real place or race is claimed.
Building grammar from Cotswold villages in general (coursed limestone, stone-slate roofs, mullioned windows, market halls, wool churches), none copied.

Textures from Poly Haven (CC0), read from the main checkout's local library (assets/polyhaven), nothing downloaded for this track: asphalt_track, japanese_stone_wall
(regraded to honey in tex/drystone_diff.jpg by make_textures.py), leafy_grass, pavement_01, gravel_ground_01, medieval_blocks_03, seaworn_stone_tiles, roof_slates_02,
grey_roof_01, white_plaster_02, weathered_planks, clay_roof_tiles_02, forest_leaves_02, dirty_concrete, rusty_corrugated_iron, painted_metal_shutter, rusted_shutter,
brushed_concrete; the review renders' skies are qwantani_noon_puresky and qwantani_dusk_1_puresky.

Trees and hedges: Qingshan's conversions of Poly Haven's scanned trees and shrubs (CC0; tracks/qingshan/make_trees.py, make_shrubs.py), loaded as they are.
Reused from other tracks: kerb paint, tyre wall and stand crowd sheet (tracks/qingshan/tex), catch fence and start lights (tracks/llano/tex).

## Generated art (ours)
- make_textures.py: the painted boards of the inn and the shops (invented names).
- make_decor_tex.py: the sponsor hoardings and the distance and corner boards, from the marks in assets/sponsors (manifest.json has each mark's source;
  this local fan prototype only, as the other tracks' boards), and the Union Flag.
