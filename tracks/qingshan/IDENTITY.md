# Cheongsan Park: identity brief

Internal id, folders and files stay `qingshan`: saved best laps and the career calendar key on it.

Every part of a lap should hold something that could only be here. Cheongsan (청산, "green mountain") is a fictional circuit in a Korean energy park: forest and water, the machines that run on them, and old imperial buildings among it all. No real building is copied, no real race is claimed. The user's direction, 2026-11-04: first "track decor [china eco specific] and ads", then that the map is in Korea. Offered a rebuild of the buildings or name and boards only, the user chose name and boards only: **the halls, pagoda, pavilion, stone gate, columns, urns, lanterns and bamboo were kept on purpose**, and they remain Chinese in style.

## What it is made of
- **The kept buildings:** yellow glazed tile, red lacquer, painted beams, pale terrace stone, bamboo, paper lanterns. Real forms only.
- **Korea:** the name, every sign in Hangul and English, Korean brands and invented Korean sponsors.
- **Eco:** turbines, solar tables, the grid they feed, a wetland, planted roofs. The working parts of an energy park, not symbols of one.
- **Not used:** painted dragons, cartoon clouds, decorative patterns drawn by script. Stone lions are left out too: there is no way to make one that isn't a caricature without a real scan.

## The lap, section by section
1. **Start and pits:** the name on the gantry in Hangul and English, solar on the pit roof, a planted hoarding line.
2. **The halls:** stone gate at the court, stone columns and bronze urns in front of the terraces, lantern posts and tall banners along the road, bamboo behind.
3. **The stack:** bamboo in drifts at the forest edge, corner names in Hangul and English.
4. **Wind farm:** a 52 m blade on cradles by the visitor hall, a wind mast, the battery store beside the substation, pylons walking away to the hills.
5. **The top straight:** "powered by wind and sun" on the gantry, a pagoda over the trees on your left.
6. **The lake:** a pavilion on a zigzag walkway, a timber boardwalk through the reeds.
7. **Solar and the stands:** the arrays, then the sponsors' run past the stands.

## Ads
Real marks as on the other tracks' boards, for this local fan prototype only: Samsung and LG (the headline), Sony, Pirelli, HSBC, Rolex, Red Bull. Invented local sponsors, all fictional and not checked against trademark registers: 새바람에너지 Saebaram Wind, 은빛호수 솔라 Eunbit Solar, 초록길 모터스 Chorokgil EV, 대숲 Daesup. One public line, 맑은 물 푸른 산 "clear water, green hills". Hoardings stand behind the wall on the straights and into the stops, on the stands' fronts and on the pit fascia. None on the hairpin stack's forest side, where the trees are the point.

Thirteen big billboards, 20 to 30 m wide, stand at irregular spots behind the barrier (the user, 2026-11-04): Samsung, LG, Hyundai, Kia, Lotte Mart. On a corner a board only stands on the outside, never on the open infield's rail, and the trees just upstream are cleared so it can be read.

## Rules
- Generated art only for signs and sponsor boards, on plain colour.
- No flicker, no blinking, no lit shell: day and dusk only.
- Nothing against the glass or across a driver's reveal of the red roof.
