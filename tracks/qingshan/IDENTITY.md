# Qingshan Park: identity brief

Every part of a lap should hold something that could only be here. Qingshan (青山, "green mountain") is a fictional circuit in a Chinese energy park: forest and water, the machines that run on them, and old imperial buildings among it all. No real building is copied, no real race is claimed. The user's direction, 2026-11-04: "track decor [china eco specific] and ads".

## What it is made of
- **China:** yellow glazed tile, red lacquer, painted beams, pale terrace stone, bamboo, paper lanterns, Chinese signage. Real forms only.
- **Eco:** turbines, solar tables, the grid they feed, a wetland, planted roofs. The working parts of an energy park, not symbols of one.
- **Not used:** painted dragons, cartoon clouds, decorative patterns drawn by script. Stone lions are left out too: there is no way to make one that isn't a caricature without a real scan.

## The lap, section by section
1. **Start and pits:** the name on the gantry in both languages, solar on the pit roof, a planted hoarding line.
2. **The halls:** stone gate at the court, stone columns and bronze urns in front of the terraces, lantern posts and tall banners along the road, bamboo behind.
3. **The stack:** bamboo in drifts at the forest edge, corner names in both languages.
4. **Wind farm:** a 52 m blade on cradles by the visitor hall, a wind mast, the battery store beside the substation, pylons walking away to the hills.
5. **The top straight:** "powered by wind and sun" on the gantry, a pagoda over the trees on your left.
6. **The lake:** a pavilion on a zigzag walkway, a timber boardwalk through the reeds.
7. **Solar and the stands:** the arrays, then the sponsors' run past the stands.

## Ads
Real marks as on the other tracks' boards, for this local fan prototype only: OPPO and vivo (Chinese brands, the headline), Sony, Pirelli, HSBC, Rolex, Red Bull. Invented local sponsors, all fictional: 青风能源 Qingfeng Wind, 碧湖光伏 Bihu Solar, 绿途电动 Lütu EV, 竹源 Zhuyuan. One public line, 绿水青山 "clear water, green mountains". Hoardings stand behind the wall on the straights and into the stops, on the stands' fronts and on the pit fascia. None on the hairpin stack's forest side, where the trees are the point.

## Rules
- Generated art only for signs and sponsor boards, on plain colour.
- No flicker, no blinking, no lit shell: day and dusk only.
- Nothing against the glass or across a driver's reveal of the red roof.
